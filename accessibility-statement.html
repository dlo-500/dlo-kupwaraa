Accessibility Statement

District Litigation Office, Kupwara · Last reviewed: October 2026

1. Commitment

The District Litigation Office, Kupwara aims to make this website usable by the widest possible audience, including persons with disabilities, in the spirit of the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 and the Guidelines for Indian Government Websites (GIGW).

2. Measures in place

  • Responsive layout for different screen sizes.
  • Text-size controls in the site toolbar.
  • Language options (English, Hindi, Urdu) for key interface text.
  • Skip link to main content on pages that use the shared site chrome.
  • Visible focus styles on interactive controls where supported by the theme.

3. Known limitations

Some complex case tables, maps, or third-party embeds may not yet meet every WCAG 2.1 Level AA criterion. We continue to improve structure, contrast, and keyboard support.

4. Feedback

If you face difficulty accessing any part of this website, please write through the Contact or Public Enquiries page with the page address and a short description of the problem. The Office will endeavour to provide the information in an accessible manner.

5. Date

This statement was last reviewed in October 2026.
