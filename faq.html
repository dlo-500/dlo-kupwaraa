Help & FAQ

District Litigation Office, Kupwara · Last reviewed: October 2026

Short answers to common questions about this coordination portal. For official records, always verify with the Office or the competent court.

What is this website for?

It helps the District Litigation Office monitor government cases, hearings, and related coordination. It is not a substitute for certified court records.

How do I search a case?

Open Case Search, or use the assistant on supported pages. You can search by party name, case number/CNR, court, or department.

Why might dates or status differ from the court?

Registry data is updated for office monitoring and may lag behind the latest court order. Always confirm critical dates from official sources.

Can the public rely on this site in court or for RTI replies?

No. Use it for reference only. Obtain certified copies and official communications from the proper authority.

How do I contact the Office?

Use the Contact page or Public Enquiries form. Personal mobile numbers of officials are not published online.

How do language options work?

Use the globe control in the toolbar to switch interface text among English, Hindi, and Urdu. Case titles and numbers remain as recorded.

I found an accessibility problem. What should I do?

See the Accessibility Statement and write to us via Contact with the page link and a short description of the issue.
