Hyperlinking Policy

District Litigation Office, Kupwara · Last reviewed: October 2026

1. Links to this website

Other websites may link to the homepage or other public pages of this site for non-commercial informational purposes, provided the link does not:

  • Suggest endorsement or partnership where none exists;
  • Misrepresent the Office’s mandate or content;
  • Frame this site’s pages in a manner that obscures its origin; or
  • Violate any applicable law.

2. Links from this website

This site may link to websites of courts, government departments, or other organisations for convenience. Once you leave this site, the linked site’s own terms and privacy practices apply. The Office is not responsible for the availability or accuracy of external sites.

3. Prior permission

Deep-linking into non-public or authenticated areas is not permitted. Requests for formal linking arrangements or use of official marks should be sent through the Contact page.

4. Removal of links

The Office may request removal of any link that is misleading, unlawful, or inconsistent with this policy.
