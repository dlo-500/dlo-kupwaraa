Privacy Policy

District Litigation Office, Kupwara · Last reviewed: October 2026

1. Purpose

This Privacy Policy describes how the District Litigation Office, Kupwara (“the Office”) handles information collected through this website and related web application.

2. Information we process

  • Case registry data used for departmental coordination (case titles, CNR/case numbers, courts, departments, hearing dates, and related procedural fields).
  • Enquiry or contact details that you voluntarily submit (for example name, email, mobile number, and message text).
  • Technical logs that may include browser type, approximate access time, and security-related events. We do not use the site to collect payment or bank details.

3. Purpose of use

Information is used only to support litigation monitoring, staff coordination, respond to legitimate enquiries, improve service reliability, and maintain security of the system.

4. Sharing

Case and enquiry information may be accessible to authorised personnel of the Office and, where required, to parent departments or competent authorities. Data is not sold. Disclosure may occur if required by law or lawful order.

5. Cookies and analytics

The site may use essential technical storage (for example language preference, theme, or session continuity) and limited analytics. These are used for operation and improvement, not for commercial profiling.

6. Data security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are applied. No internet transmission can be guaranteed as fully secure. Users should avoid sending highly sensitive personal information through public forms unless necessary.

7. Your responsibilities

Do not submit another person’s personal data unless you are authorised to do so. Content on this site is for coordination and reference; official records must be verified with the Office.

8. Contact

For privacy-related queries, use the Contact page or write to the District Litigation Officer, Kupwara.
